Academic · Sustainability finance Summer 2026

Project YR-Blue

Can marine restoration become a carbon asset Yamaha can defend?

Honorable mention in Georgia Tech’s Carbon Reduction Challenge, with Yamaha Rightwaters. The brief maps where restoration is safe, whether the carbon pays back, and whether robotics beats manual diving.

Honorable mention Partner: Yamaha
PythonOSMnxRandom ForestNPV
Open the brief

The decision

Where to restore seagrass, at what scale the voluntary carbon math breaks even, and whether Yamaha’s Water Strider vessel is operationally faster than divers.

How it was built

Three notebooks. A suitability engine uses OpenStreetMap and NOAA context to score shipping lanes, ports, depth, and sea-surface conditions, then a Random Forest to flag expansion sites. A 10-year NPV compares a 100-hectare pilot with a 1,000-hectare phase, with weather loss and a saltwater maintenance cost. A simulator times robotic deployment against manual diving, including a 5 cm operating draft.

Stakeholder takeaway

Carbon neutrality stops being a slogan when restoration has a risk map, a payback, and an operating model. Exact pilot coordinates stay out of the public repo under the project NDA.

Hackathon · SproutGT 2026 April 2026

Predictive runway via peer cohorts

How long does a finance startup actually last when the founder has no history?

Built at SproutGT for accounting and insurance founders. Peer companies stand in for the history a new firm does not have. A tree model finds the step-cost. An LLM judges that variance instead of offering generic advice.

500+ synthetic peers Cold-start forecast
XGBoostGeminiFigmaPython
Open the brief

The decision

How much runway to believe, after regulatory cost and local customer-acquisition pressure, before the first operating dollar is spent.

How it was built

A Python cohort of more than 500 synthetic accounting and insurance firms, split by region, size, and domain. Gradient boosting compares a founder’s optimistic burn with the peer burn. Gemini 1.5 Flash receives that gap and writes a senior-partner verdict. Figma carries a similarity radar and a reality-check view. The adjusted runway is capital divided by peer burn, widened by the variance.

Stakeholder takeaway

Collective history is a privacy-preserving substitute for a track record. The next version wants live, anonymized books — Plaid or QuickBooks — in place of the synthetic set.
